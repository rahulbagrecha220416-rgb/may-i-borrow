## Shared copy (paste into any form)

**Name:** May We Borrow

**Tagline (60 chars):** Borrow from your building instead of buying

**Short description (160 chars):**
A free lending circle for apartment blocks and clubs. List what you own, borrow from neighbours
you already know. No ads, no marketplace, open source.

**Long description:**
May We Borrow is a free, open-source app for groups of people who already know each other: an
apartment tower, a cycling club, an office floor. Create a group, share the invite link on
WhatsApp, and members list what they're happy to lend: ladders, drills, mixers, board games,
camera gear. Ask to borrow, the owner approves, and the app tracks who has what and when it's
due back.

Items are only visible inside your groups, so there's no marketplace and no strangers. Lending
earns karma, every member signs a short pledge to look after what they borrow, and groups can be
run by admins or by member vote. Try it in ten seconds with "Continue as Guest", no sign-up.

Built as a community service. No ads, no tracking, not a business.
