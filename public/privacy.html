Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

May We Borrow is a free, open-source community app for lending things within groups of people who already know each other. It has no ads and is not a business. This page explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and profile photo, from Google when you sign in with Google, or the email you register with.
  • What you add: groups you create or join, items you list (name, description, photos, pickup address, pickup time and contact), borrow requests, messages to lenders or mutual friends, and votes on group proposals.
  • Notifications: if you allow push notifications in the Android app, a device token so we can send them, and your notification settings.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we do not collect your location.

Who can see it

  • Your name, photo and the items you list are visible to members of the groups you share them with. Item pickup details are shown to people in those groups so a loan can be arranged.
  • Borrow requests and messages are visible to the people they are sent to.
  • We never sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Services that store it for us

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and photo storage.
  • Vercel: hosts the website.
  • Google: Google sign-in, and push notifications on Android (Firebase Cloud Messaging).

These providers process data only to run the app.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your account and content while you use the app. You can remove your items yourself at any time. To delete your whole account and everything linked to it, email hello@mayweborrow.com from the address you signed in with. We delete it within 30 days.

Age

May We Borrow is for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page.
